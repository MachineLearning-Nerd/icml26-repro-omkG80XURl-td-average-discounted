# Method — Claim 1

A two-state irreducible/aperiodic family has phi=(-1,1), flip probability eta/2, bounded symmetric reward noise, and theta*=1. A 3x3 affine moment operator is exponentiated exactly, not sampled. Route 1 preregisters an equality-style exponent fit. Route 2 treats tilde-O as the upper bound it is: a quadratic envelope is calibrated on eta>=0.20 and tested without refitting on smaller held-out eta, while an eta^-1 envelope is required to fail as a negative control. A separate Appendix-G-scale sweep checks external validity at (n,d)=(50,5),(100,20),(1000,100).
